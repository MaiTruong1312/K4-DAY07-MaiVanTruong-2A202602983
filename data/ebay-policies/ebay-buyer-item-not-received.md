# Get help with an item that has not arrived

If the estimated delivery date has passed and an order has not arrived, the buyer can report that the item was not received. Before reporting it, the buyer can check the expected delivery date, shipping address, and tracking updates in **Purchases**.

If the checkout address was incorrect, the buyer can ask the seller to cancel and relist the item. A seller cannot cancel after shipping. If tracking says delivered, eBay recommends checking whether a neighbor accepted the parcel.

## Reporting a missing item

The buyer can request an order update, replacement, or refund to the original payment method. Most sellers respond within **3 business days**. To remain eligible for buyer protection, the buyer must report the missing item within **30 calendar days after the delivery date**. Refunds are typically available within **3–5 business days**.

To report through My eBay:

1. Find the item in **Purchases**.
2. Select **More actions**, then **I didn't receive it**.
3. Choose either a refund or the item.
4. Add information and submit the request.

If part of a set or bundle is missing, the buyer must open a return request instead. The buyer may return the set for a full refund, or the seller may offer a partial refund while allowing the buyer to keep what arrived.

## Asking eBay to step in

If the seller has not responded or resolved the problem after **3 business days**, the buyer may ask eBay to intervene. This request must be made within **21 business days** after the original request was opened. eBay normally returns a decision within **48 hours**. An appeal with new information may be filed within **30 calendar days** after the case closes.

If the order later arrives, the buyer can close the request. A closed request or case cannot be reopened. If there is a problem with the arrived item, the buyer must close the missing-item request before opening a return request.
