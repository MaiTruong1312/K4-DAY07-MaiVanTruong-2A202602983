# Returns, items not received, and refunds for buyers

When an item does not arrive or needs to be returned, a buyer should:

1. Open a request from **Purchase history** to tell the seller.
2. Work with the seller, who is expected to respond within **3 business days**.
3. Ask eBay to step in if the seller does not respond or the issue remains unresolved.

If the seller does not honor the return policy stated in the listing, the buyer may qualify for the eBay Money Back Guarantee. A buyer who purchased as a guest can still request a return or report a missing item.

For a change-of-mind return, the seller's listing determines whether returns are accepted. If an item arrives damaged, faulty, or not as described, most purchases can be returned for a refund under the eBay Money Back Guarantee even when the seller does not accept returns.

After a seller accepts a return, the buyer must send the item back. Who pays return shipping depends on the return reason and the seller's policy.

If the seller has not responded after **3 business days** or cannot resolve the issue, the buyer may ask eBay to intervene. A buyer who disagrees with eBay's decision can appeal and should provide additional evidence, such as photographs of the problem.
