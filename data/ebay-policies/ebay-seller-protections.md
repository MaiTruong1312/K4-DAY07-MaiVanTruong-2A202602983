# Seller protections

eBay protects eligible sellers from abusive buyer behavior and events outside their control. When eBay confirms abusive behavior, it may remove associated negative or neutral feedback, defects, and opened cases from service metrics. It may also restrict the buyer's ability to request returns or suspend the buyer for serious or repeated abuse.

## Top Rated Seller protection

These protections require the seller to be Top Rated when protection applies, reside in the United States (including territories) or Canada, have no **Very High** service metric, list the item on eBay.com, and offer returns of at least **30 days**.

If eBay confirms that a buyer falsely claimed an item was not as described, it may subsidize up to **$6.00** of the return-label cost. The credit may take up to **60 days**. Related negative or neutral feedback is removed and the return does not count toward the seller's not-as-described rate.

## Used or damaged returns

When an accepted return arrives used, altered, or damaged, an eligible seller may deduct up to **50%** from the refund to recover lost value. The seller must report the buyer and send the refund within **2 business days** after receiving the item.

The seller receives no fee credits when making this deduction, and the option disappears if eBay steps in. If eBay is asked to intervene after the refund, the case does not count as closed without seller resolution; eBay may keep the return open for up to **10 days** while working with the buyer.

Sellers who are not Below Standard can receive this used-or-damaged-return protection on listings that offer free returns.

## Events outside the seller's control

eBay may remove defects or feedback caused by weather, carrier disruption, or late arrival when tracking shows the seller shipped on time. Sellers can also be protected when a buyer retracts a bid, fails to pay, or demands terms that were not offered in the original listing. Sellers should keep relevant communication in eBay Messages and report abusive buyers so eBay can investigate.
