# Ask eBay to step in and help for sellers

A seller who cannot resolve a buyer's return or purchase issue can ask eBay to intervene. Once eBay steps in, it normally responds to both parties within **48 hours**.

The seller has **3 business days** after the buyer reports the issue to provide a resolution. After that period, either party may ask eBay to step in. If they continue negotiating, they generally have up to **21 business days** after the request opened to ask for help.

If eBay intervenes at any stage of a return and the item comes back used or damaged, the seller can no longer deduct an amount from the refund, even if seller protections would otherwise apply.

## Requesting intervention

From Seller Hub, open **Orders**, then the **Returns** or **Requests and disputes** tab. Select **See details**, then **Report problem**. For an item-not-received request in My eBay Orders, open **View request details** and select **Ask eBay to step in and help**.

eBay may ask the buyer to return the item when it cannot determine that the item matches the listing, the seller's stated return policy applies, or the seller already offered to accept the return.

If eBay decides the seller met their obligations, the case closes without further action. If eBay decides the seller owes a refund, eBay issues it and seeks reimbursement from the seller. The seller receives no fee credits, and the case counts as closed without seller resolution, which may affect seller performance.

A seller can appeal by supplying new information within **30 calendar days** after the case closes.
